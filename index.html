Before your visit.

Not sure which service to choose?+

Start with Signature gel for color on your natural nails, Sculpted extensions for added length, or Bespoke nail art for a detailed custom design. You can describe what you have in mind in your appointment brief.

Can I bring an inspiration photo?+

Absolutely. Save the looks you love and bring them to your consultation. Your stylist can use them as a starting point to create a set that suits your nail shape and personal style.

How does the appointment request work?+

Choose a service, select a preferred day, and add your inspiration. This concept site creates a brief you can copy or download. Requests are not sent, and no appointment is reserved until the stylist confirms it.

What if I already have a set on?+

Include your current nail product and whether you need removal in the design notes. This helps your stylist plan the right amount of time and explain any extra cost before your appointment.
